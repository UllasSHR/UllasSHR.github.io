I'm Ullas, a CS student.

My recent work has focused on databases and realtime applications. In
[Last Seat Lab](/posts/last-seat-lab/), I used one shared seat to explore competing
reservations, transactions, caller identity, and what a browser can honestly show when
its connection drops.

That led me into SpacetimeDB's source. I independently
[tested a WebSocket error-handling change](https://github.com/clockworklabs/SpacetimeDB/pull/5707#issuecomment-5382007539)
and opened a [PostgreSQL logging pull request](https://github.com/clockworklabs/SpacetimeDB/pull/5882).
I also proposed a [CLI fix in Evalite](https://github.com/mattpocock/evalite/pull/403).

I use Codex as my daily driver. I want to understand the important paths, check the
results, and be able to explain the decisions myself.

## Selected work

- **[Last Seat Lab](/posts/last-seat-lab/)** — experiments in transactions,
  authorization, durability, and realtime state through a shared seat-reservation system.
- **Other experiments** — I've explored different ideas through an
  [AI-news filter](/posts/ai-ingest/), a [shared health-ritual app](/posts/breathe/),
  and a [launch-risk scanner](/posts/launchguard/), trying different approaches
  to turning an idea into something people can use.

Outside the terminal, I like photographs, travel, looking at the sky, and being present
with the people I care about. I wrote more about that in
[From nineteen to twenty](/posts/from-nineteen-to-twenty/).
