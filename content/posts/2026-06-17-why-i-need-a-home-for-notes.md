I want a place for the things that are too long, too rough, or too useful to disappear into a timeline.

Some posts will be about projects I am building. Some will be notes from a day in my life. Some will be tiny references I keep needing again. The rule is simple: if something is worth saving and maybe useful to one other person, it can live here.

This site is not meant to be a perfect blog. It is a public notebook.

## What I Want To Post Here

- Builder logs from projects I am shipping.
- What broke and how I fixed it.
- Small thoughts that need more room than a tweet.
- Things I learned from using AI tools.
- Personal notes from ordinary days.

The goal is not to sound polished. The goal is to leave proof that I am noticing, building, and learning.
