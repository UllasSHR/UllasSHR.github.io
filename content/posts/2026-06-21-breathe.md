Breathe is a two-person health ritual app.

The goal is not to make another complicated health dashboard. The goal is to make health feel like a small daily rhythm: track a few things, get useful activities, keep a streak, and do it with a partner.

[GitHub repo](https://github.com/UllasSHR/Breathe)  
[Live app](https://breathe-sand.vercel.app)

## Small README

Breathe is built with Next.js, Supabase, Google Fit, Gemini, Resend, and web push.

The app includes:

- magic-link login,
- onboarding,
- medical record entry and PDF extraction,
- daily activity generation,
- Google Fit syncing,
- push reminders,
- streaks,
- a partner leaderboard.

The important product idea is simple: health tracking works better when it becomes a shared ritual instead of a solo spreadsheet.

## How To Use

Locally, the repo runs like a normal Next.js app:

```sh
npm install
npm run dev
```

The full setup needs Supabase, environment variables, Google Fit OAuth, Gemini, Resend, push notification keys, and the database migrations from the repo.

As a user, the flow is:

1. Sign in with a magic link.
2. Complete onboarding.
3. Add or upload health records.
4. Generate daily activities.
5. Connect Google Fit and push notifications.
6. Use the dashboard and leaderboard as the daily habit loop.

## What I Learned

Breathe taught me how many real systems are involved once an app becomes more than a landing page.

It touched auth, database design, row-level security, file extraction, AI suggestions, OAuth, push notifications, cron jobs, and deployment.

The biggest lesson was that finishing a product means connecting many boring pieces correctly. The app idea matters, but the proof is whether the whole loop works end to end.

## Why It Matters

Breathe matters to me because it was a finished app, not just an experiment.

It proved that I can build a full product workflow with real users, real data, and real daily behavior.
