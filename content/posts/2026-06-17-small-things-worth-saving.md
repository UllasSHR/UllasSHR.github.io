---
title: "Small Things Worth Saving"
date: "2026-06-17"
summary: "A simple posting rule for a personal site: save the things that would otherwise vanish."
tags: ["life", "notes"]
---

Not every thought has to become a polished essay.

Some ideas are just small things worth saving:

- a lesson from a bug,
- a line from a conversation,
- a setup command I will forget,
- a feeling from a strange day,
- a tiny project that almost worked.

Social media is good for quick sharing, but it is not always good for memory. This site can be the slower place where the useful bits stay findable.

That is enough reason to keep it.
